Flow 01 — Entry

Splash → USP carousel → Login → Secure account

Shared by all three roles. Dealer, Chemist and MR authenticate through the same door — the role tab picked at login determines which journey the OTP routes into. Mobile entry and OTP verification are separate screens; after verification the user chooses PIN or biometric, then lands on a shared success screen before Home.

Flow 02 — MR journey

Dashboard → onboard a new partner → status tracking

The dashboard carries a header (MR name, ID, zone) and a footer nav, since it's the MR's home base. Onboarded/pending are the only two stats, color-coded; onboarding is a three-step editable wizard; submission ends on a nudge toward the monthly target instead of a flat confirmation; and the tracker uses an icon timeline with a partner summary card.
